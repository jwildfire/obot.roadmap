obot

Jeremy Wildfire's open-source safety-graphics modernization portfolio — planned, tracked, and largely built with AI agents in the loop.

The portfolio modernizes the SafetyGraphics interactive clinical-safety displays — nine archived renderers built on decade-old JavaScript — into a single maintained charting library with requirement-traceable tests, consumed from R the same way gsm.kri consumes gsm.viz. Work is tracked as objective, requirement and task issues under the issue contract and implemented in the repos below.

How work happens

An objective is an issue tree: requirements under it, tasks under those, every node with a definition of done and a milestone. A requirement's status is one status: label — backlog → ready → in session → review → released — moved by the session that runs it; the tracker reads the labels and the sub-issue links, and the project board is retired. A cloud session runs one requirement at a time, steered by @jwildfire through the issues; the three documents under docs/ are the standards every session works under. Design documents live under requirements/design/; agents draft, he signs off.

History

From May to June 2026 this portfolio ran as "Open Source OrangeBot" on the obot-claw hub — an autonomous-agent experiment with nightly briefings, whose story is told in the framework report. The hub was archived in July 2026 and its diary and reports were migrated here. From July to September 2026 a second, fully autonomous prototype ran from obot.agent; it was retired on 2026-09-10 in favour of requirement sessions, for the reasons recorded in the ways of working.

Maintained in jwildfire/obot.roadmap · built with AI assistance · the tracker, news and metrics regenerate daily.
