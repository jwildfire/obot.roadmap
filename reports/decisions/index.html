The contract

  • One folder per decision at reports/decisions/{YYYY-MM-DD}-{slug}/, self-contained HTML plus a README.md recording provenance, sources and assumptions.
  • Linked from the blocked goal's hub issue, and surfaced in that night's executive summary under Critical blockers.
  • Posted to the hub's Q&A discussions as a brief executive summary — question, options, recommendation — linking the artifact for the full argument. The thread is where @jwildfire documents the decision.
  • One artifact per decision topic — a single page carries several decisions only when they gate each other and must be answered in one sitting.
  • Full contract: decisions/README.md.
