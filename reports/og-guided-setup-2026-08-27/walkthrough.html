    §Notes on the mock

    What is real

    • Every sample row, every column name, all 16 analyte spellings, the 1,005 / 57,200 / 765 counts, the overlap table, the candidate ranking and the “0 of 4” hepatic match — all measured. Sample rows are committed at data/delivery.js.
    • The source_col: spec on step 8 is the one that was actually executed through gsm.mapping::ApplySpec(), producing 57,200 mapped rows over 765 participants.
    • The ledger's consequences come from the workflow dependency graph in demo-301/workflows/, resolved through Mapped_* to raw domains.

    What is invented

    • The visual design of the interview. open.gismo has no setup route today; this borrows the product's espresso palette so the mock looks like the product rather than like the report.
    • The chart on step 4 is a static SVG drawn from the real ALT distribution's shape, not a live safety.viz render.
    • config/setup-log.md does not exist. It is proposed here as the artefact that keeps the plain-English record next to the machine-readable one.

    What the mock deliberately does not hide

    • Step 6 has no good answer and the mock does not invent one. The absent toxicity grade is the point at which this direction runs out of things to ask, and the screen says so in the user's own words rather than dressing it as a choice.
    • Step 7's answer has nowhere to go. The mock shows it writing to a module YAML, which is a patch, not a seam.
