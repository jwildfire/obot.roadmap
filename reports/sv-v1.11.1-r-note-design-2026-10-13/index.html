A standard note where R can be started

  • Every chart and tab that can start R carries one note at the top of its card, and Start R is a button inside the note's sentence.
  • The note is an amber warning where nothing draws without R, and a blue information note where the chart draws and R adds its p-values.
  • The line about the data on first open is removed. Blue becomes the colour of information in the notes that remain, and none keeps a purple edge.

Decisions for you

Five, each with the recommended answer first. The mockups below show the recommended answers; the first mockup has a switch for each alternative that changes the page.

Round two, 13 October: you read the first round and said "mock-up looks good. Get rid of the data info box." The line on first open is now removed in every mockup, and the five answers stay as recommended until you approve them.

  1. Where the note sits on a chart that draws without R

    • RecommendedAt the top of the chart's card: the same place on every chart and on the RBQM tab, seen without scrolling, and built in safety.viz alone.
    • AlternativeIn place of the line under the chart, where the p-value will be printed. That line is drawn by bio.viz, so it needs a bio.viz release too, and on a tall chart it is below the first screen. Mockup 1, "Note under the chart".

  2. The control at the top right, before R is started

    • RecommendedKept as it is. A reader can then close a blue note and still start R later, and the control stays the one place for progress, the R chip and Why.
    • AlternativeHidden until R is started, so there is one Start R on screen. The blue note then has no cross. Mockup 1, "No control until R starts".

  3. Which charts get the warning

    • RecommendedThe RBQM tab, the Biomarker screen and the Correlation matrix: none of them draws a result without R. Group comparison, Association scatter and Cross-tabulation get the blue note.
    • AlternativeThe warning on the RBQM tab only, and the blue note on all five biomarker charts.

  4. The app's other notes

    • RecommendedOne rule for every note: blue is information, amber is something that cannot run until the reader acts, red is something that failed. So a chart that needs more data, and an RBQM metric the loaded data cannot support, become amber; the rest become blue. The table under "Every note in the app" lists each one.
    • AlternativeSwap purple for blue and change nothing else.

  5. When R does not start

    • RecommendedThe note turns amber, with Try again in its sentence. The chart still stands, and the reader can act.
    • AlternativeRed, as the app's "Did not draw" lines are.

The sentences in the notes are drafts for you to correct. Buttons and links outside a note keep the app's purple: it is the colour of things to press, and only notes change.

The note

One component, drawn here by the page itself in the proposed styles, so the text is live. It has two formats, and the same shape in both: a mark, one sentence with at most one button in it, and a cross only where closing it loses nothing.

  • Once R is ready there is no note. The R chip at the top right says R is running, as today.
  • The mark's shape differs between the formats, so the two can be told apart without colour.
  • Blue is the palette's own blue, and amber its own amber: no colour is added to the app.
  • On a phone the sentence wraps under the mark and the button stays inside it. Phone width is not mocked here; it is tested in the build.

How the app knows which format a chart gets

  • For this release, the page's list of libraries names the two biomarker charts that cannot be drawn without R; every other chart of a library that can start R gets the blue note.
  • The better home is each chart's own entry in bio.viz's chart list. That needs a bio.viz release, so it is left as a follow-up.
  • The RBQM tab is the app's own, and says it itself.

Left out

  • The note inside bio.viz's own line under the chart, unless you choose the alternative in decision 1.
  • A note on a chart outside the demo app: the docs site's gallery pages and the gsm.safety widgets start no R.
  • Any change to the control at the top right, unless you choose the alternative in decision 2.
  • A replacement for the line on first open. The Data tab's name tag and the Data tab itself say whose data is loaded; nothing else is added.

Not verified in these mockups

  • Phone width. The mockups are desktop only.
  • The Group comparison with one biomarker open, where the tests are printed. The mockup shows its first view, the tiles.
  • That the copy of bio.viz in the app redraws its own line when the sentence handed to it changes. It prints the sentence the app hands it today, so no bio.viz change is expected.
