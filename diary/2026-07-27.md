## Work completed

**The research ([reports/csr-change-request-framework-2026-07-27](https://jwildfire.github.io/obot.roadmap/reports/csr-change-request-framework-2026-07-27/)).** Four Opus 5 research lanes — commercial CSR tools, pharma document-review platforms, ARS/pharmaverse standards state, and cross-domain change-workflow patterns — synthesized by the Fable lead into [Part 1, the landscape](https://jwildfire.github.io/obot.roadmap/reports/csr-change-request-framework-2026-07-27/landscape.html): 18 products deep-dived on the change-request seam. The seam held and sharpened: none tracks a TLF change request as an object, none re-flags prose when a table's numbers move, and the vendors concede it themselves — TriloDocs works "much like a photocopier," Narrativa's automation "shortens response times to medical writer change requests," Merck's internal system consumes "immutable TLFs." The load-bearing market fact: most of a 12-week CSR cycle is cross-functional review (J&J, AMWA 2025), and the entire 2025–26 product wave optimizes the other end. New findings the kickoff survey lacked: **Clymbr Hub** (Feb 2026) is the competitor to watch — ARS-native shells, generated programs, a TLF review product with e-signature, and shell versioning as their Enterprise paywall — but missing the comment→spec reverse edge; `cards` 0.8.0 shipped **`compare_ard()`**, the value-level diff primitive the loop needs; CDISC **ARS has no lifecycle model** (verified against the model source; the gap is a three-year-old open issue); the as-is TLF revision loop reconstructed in eleven sourced steps, whose change counter is an Excel column called Round Number.

**The framework ([Part 2](https://jwildfire.github.io/obot.roadmap/reports/csr-change-request-framework-2026-07-27/framework.html)).** Five pillars, each with named prior art: the change request as a versioned artifact (`requests/CR-NNN.yaml`, verbatim request + agent assessment + derived state); **review tier proven by the computed ARD diff** — a presentation change is cosmetic because the ARD hash is unchanged, an analysis change puts the value-level diff in front of the statistician (the industry's own signed-SAP/unsigned-shell asymmetry made mechanical, with EMA §2.3.3.3's post-hoc rule encoded and E3 §9.8 notes auto-drafted); version-vs-fork rules; drafts rendered watermarked in the pr/{N} preview while current stays live, with an Atlantis-style staleness guard on promotion; and **dual sign-off** — the biostatistician signs the spec+ARD diff, the medical writer signs the binding impact report (every sentence whose number moved), requester never approves, agent never approves. Five shippable increments, composer-first, platform-graduation last, honoring the §12 review-layer deferral.
